quota verification for non-serving synthetic prototypes, accepting possible PC
freeze, disk exhaustion and disruption. The associated capability inspection was
tool-denied; no delegation or aggregate containment was proved. Those checkpoints
and attempt histories remain in BACKLOG.md and AGENTS.md.

archive it … all the others are ok.” Item 4 was the personal-PC resource-limit
decision. Aggregate quota provisioning/checks are therefore retired as a prerequisite
for personal local text chat, including its local-serving acceptance. Do not schedule
another quota inspection or infer that quotas were installed or passed.

Practical request/output/time/concurrency bounds, filesystem/project/credential and
network isolation remain required. Attachment processing and later shared-server
resource requirements are unchanged. This exception accepts resource exhaustion;
it does not permit unconfined execution or waive a reproduced security/data defect.
